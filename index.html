STL-STAMPER // STAMP 2D ART ONTO 3D MODELS

 1. LOAD AN .STL OR .3MF (F2) AND AN .SVG/.PNG/.JPG (F3).
    A 3MF WITH SEVERAL PARTS SHOWS THEM AS A LIST:
    CLICK A PART TO INCLUDE / EXCLUDE IT FROM THE WORK,
    DEL REMOVES IT (ALSO FROM THE EXPORTED 3MF).
    HOVER A PART TO SEE IT OUTLINED IN THE VIEWPORT.
 2. DRAG THE STAMP TO SLIDE IT OVER THE SURFACE, OR
    DOUBLE-CLICK THE MODEL TO DROP IT THERE. USE THE
    FACE BUTTONS / FLIP TO JUMP TO ANOTHER SIDE.
 3. PICK A MODE:
    RELIEF  - RAISED, HEIGHT IN MM
    CUTOUT  - ENGRAVED TO A DEPTH, OR CUT THROUGH
    FLUSH   - LEVEL WITH THE SURFACE, SEPARATE BODY
              FOR 2-COLOR PRINTING
 4. F5 BUILDS THE REAL GEOMETRY (CSG). WITH AUTO BUILD
    ON IT RUNS BY ITSELF AFTER EACH CHANGE.
 5. EXPORT:
    3MF  - FROM AN STL: BODY + STAMP AS TWO PARTS OF ONE
           OBJECT, FILAMENT 1 AND 2 PRE-ASSIGNED.
           FROM A 3MF: THE ORIGINAL PROJECT IS KEPT
           (PRINTER/FILAMENT/PROCESS SETTINGS, PLATES,
           OTHER OBJECTS). ONLY THE TOUCHED MESHES CHANGE
           AND THE STAMP IS ADDED AS A NEW PART. IF NO
           FILAMENT MATCHES THE STAMP COLOR, ONE IS ADDED.
    STL  - EVERYTHING MERGED, OR EACH BODY ALONE.
           (STL HAS NO COLORS: USE 3MF FOR 2 COLORS.)

KEYS
 Q / E ........ ROTATE -/+ 5 DEG  (SHIFT = 1 DEG)
 [ / ] ........ SIZE -/+ 5%
 ARROWS ....... NUDGE 0.5 MM  (SHIFT = 2 MM)
 LEFT MOUSE ... DRAG THE STAMP = MOVE IT
                + SHIFT: LOCK TO THE FIRST DIRECTION MOVED
                (HORIZONTAL OR VERTICAL) UNTIL SHIFT IS UP
                DOUBLE-CLICK THE MODEL = PUT STAMP THERE
 CENTER H/V ... CENTER THE STAMP ON ITS FACE
 RIGHT MOUSE .. ORBIT CAMERA
 MIDDLE MOUSE . PAN CAMERA
 WHEEL ........ ZOOM
 CTRL+Z ....... UNDO
 CTRL+Y ....... REDO  (ALSO CTRL+SHIFT+Z)
 M ............ MIRROR
 F ............ FLIP STAMP TO THE OPPOSITE SIDE
                (E.G. TOP -> FACE ON THE PRINT BED)
 PUT ON FACE .. TOP/BOTTOM/FRONT/BACK/LEFT/RIGHT
                BUTTONS JUMP THE STAMP TO THAT SIDE
 HOME / F7 .... RESET VIEW
 F10 .......... CRT FX: LITE / FULL WARP / OFF
 ESC .......... CLOSE THIS
